# PewPew privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

PewPew is developed by Muhammed Saeed. This policy describes the iPhone game's data use.

## Progress and preferences

PewPew stores level scores, stars, and sound preferences on your device. These values are not sent to the developer. Removing the app can remove this data. Any device backup is controlled by your device and Apple settings.

## Accounts, advertising, and tracking

PewPew does not require an account and does not include advertising, in-app purchases, or third-party advertising trackers. The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or location.

## App updates

PewPew uses Expo's EAS Update service to check for and download compatible game updates. These requests transmit network information, including your IP address, and technical update information such as the app's platform, runtime version, and update channel to Expo and its delivery providers. This service delivers game code and assets. It does not receive your scores or saved progress.

For information about Expo's handling of data, see [Expo's privacy policy](https://expo.dev/privacy).

## Support

If you contact support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to provide. This information is used to answer your request or investigate the problem. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

## Changes and contact

This page will be updated if the game's data practices change. For questions or help, email [support@pewpew.saeed.sh](mailto:support@pewpew.saeed.sh) or visit [PewPew support](https://pewpew.saeed.sh/support).
