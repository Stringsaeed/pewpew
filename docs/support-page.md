# PewPew support

PewPew is a space arcade game with 30 levels and three bosses.

## How to play

Tap PLAY and choose an unlocked level. Drag to steer your rocket. Your rocket fires automatically. Avoid hazards and enemy fire, collect crystals, and use shield and rapid-fire pickups.

Clear a level to unlock the next one. Replay levels to improve your score and earn up to three stars.

## Sound and pausing

Open Settings to switch music and sound effects on or off. Tap the pause button during a run to pause the game.

## Progress and offline play

Your progress and sound preferences are saved on your device. Removing the app can remove this saved progress. PewPew does not currently sync progress between devices.

The game works offline. When connected, it can download game fixes and asset updates. A downloaded update normally takes effect after closing and reopening the app.
