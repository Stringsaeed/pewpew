Deliver, in order:
1. `scripts/gen-meshes.ts` (`npm run gen:meshes`): seeded, byte-identical on rerun (verify by running twice and diffing). Primitives: lathe(profile, segments, color), icosphere(subdiv, noiseAmp, rng), prism, spikes, ring. Builders for all 17 MeshIds: rocket (lathe hull with fins and a nozzle, two-tone), asteroid0..3 (noisy icospheres, grey-violet), fragment0..1, mine (sphere with spikes, red core), drone (flat wedge, cyan), well (ring, deep purple), crystal (bipyramid, mint), shield (icosphere, blue), rapid (prism, amber), wormhole (torus-ish ring, white-violet), warden, hive, core (three boss shapes, bigger, each with an obvious weak-point color). Flat shading: unshared vertices, one face normal per triangle, Float32 colors from a neon palette. Output src/meshes.gen.ts as typed arrays built from number literals (keep the file under 1.5 MB; reduce subdivisions if needed) with the exact MeshData shape and a radius per mesh. Also emit a `MESH_STATS` comment line (vertex and index totals).
2. `src/render.ts`: createRenderer(device, ctx, format) and frame(world, events, alpha, dt). Three pipelines in one render pass with depth: stars (fullscreen triangle, procedural hashed layers parallaxed by camera y, no buffers), mesh (one shared vertex and index buffer holding every MeshData interleaved pos|normal|color, one instance buffer of x,y,rot,scale,tint rgb,flash written once per frame after a counting sort by MeshId, drawIndexed per non-empty mesh with firstInstance, Lambert plus emissive vertex color, the rocket, boss and wormhole appended from World scalars), glow (instanced additive quads, no depth write, for bolts, exhaust, explosion particles, the shield bubble). Particles are renderer-owned CPU SoA with a 2048 cap, spawned from SimEvents (destroy, playerHit, pickup, shieldBlock) and continuously for exhaust. Interpolate positions by alpha between px and x. Camera: perspective, slight pitch, follows player y. Resize from clientWidth times PixelRatio inside frame; ctx.present() after every submit. No per-frame allocation in frame() apart from what WebGPU requires; reuse typed arrays.
3. Verify on the simulator with a preview harness App.tsx that builds a fake WorldView (hand-filled Bodies with a few of every kind, a boss, a wormhole) and animates it, plus fires a synthetic destroy event every second. Screenshots to .audit/render-preview-1.png and -2.png. Use debugger-log-registry to confirm zero errors and warnings over 10 s. Measure and report frame time by timing frame() over 300 frames with performance.now() and logging the average and p95 (log once, not per frame).

Done when gen:meshes is byte-identical on rerun, typecheck passes, the preview renders every mesh recognisably (describe each in your report, with the screenshot), and the frame() average is under 6 ms on the simulator. Commit the harness last and separately.
